ReadingTurn, zoom, read

The pageAs big as the screen: 2160 pixels wide on a 4KP. The title and page number show as it opens.
Page turnThe page curls over. Settings can make it a slide, or none.
ZoomStart: 2x, 3x, back. The joystick moves around; the text sharpens when you stop.
Next pages are ready before you get there: the app draws ahead while you read, so turning is instant. A scanned PDF takes longest, about a second a page.
Manga: Settings > Reading direction > Right to left swaps the flippers, so the left one turns forward. A book marked as manga in its ComicInfo.xml reads that way by itself.
B while zoomed shows the whole page again; B on the whole page goes back to the library. Your place is saved.

What's this book?Details

Press Y on a book in the library: its cover, series and issue, the story, who published it and when, the writer and artist, a summary, and how far in you are.
The details come from a ComicInfo.xml file inside the comic, the tag file most comic tools write. A book with one shows as "Daredevil Comics #1" in the library and on the DMD, not by its file name.

No ComicInfo?Look it up

  1. On the details, press X. The series and issue number are read from the name: Jumbo_Comics_082.cbr is Jumbo Comics #82.
  2. It's looked up in the Grand Comics Database (comics.org), which knows the story, date, credits and what's inside.
  3. "Is this it?" A keeps it, X tries another series with that number, B drops it. Kept details stay in the app; your file isn't changed.

The cabinet needs the internet for this. comics.org allows a few dozen lookups an hour for each home; if it asks you to wait, it says for how long. Details: Grand Comics Database, CC BY-SA 4.0.

Make it yoursSettings

Wi-Fi upload: send books from a phone or computer (next page).
Check for updates: gets the newest version from GitHub and restarts into it. Your books, places and settings stay.
Page turn: Curl, Slide or None. Reading direction: Left to right, or Right to left for manga. Change them with the joystick.
Forget reading history: clears Continue and every book's place. Redraw covers: draws every cover again.
About: the version, the license and who made what.
Comic Reader Guide5

Adding booksWi-Fi upload

  1. In Settings, open Wi-Fi upload. The cabinet shows an address and a PIN.
  2. On a phone or computer on the same network, open the address and enter the PIN.
  3. Pick Comics or Manuals, and a series (or make a new one).
  4. Drop in files, or a folder of page pictures. B on the cabinet when they're sent.

Or on the USB stick

Put books in external/comic-reader/comics/ or manuals/. A folder of books makes a series: comics/Planet Comics/Planet Comics 037.cbr

What it opens

PDF, CBZ, CBR and CBT comics, single pictures (JPG, PNG, WebP, GIF, BMP, TIFF), and a folder of pictures as one book, its pages in name order.
