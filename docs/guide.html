Adding booksWi-Fi upload

  1. In Settings, open Wi-Fi upload. The cabinet shows an address and a four-digit PIN.
  2. On a phone or computer on the same network, open the address in a browser and enter the PIN.
  3. Choose Comics or Manuals, and a series if you like (or make a new one).
  4. Drop in your files, or a folder of page pictures. Press B when they're sent: they're in your library.

Or copy themOn the USB stick

Put books in external/comic-reader/comics/ or manuals/. A folder inside makes a series:
comics/Planet Comics/Planet Comics 037.cbr

What it opensFile types

PDF, CBZ, CBR and CBT comics, single pictures (JPG, PNG, WebP, GIF, BMP, TIFF), and a folder of pictures as one book, its pages in name order. A ComicInfo.xml inside a comic gives it its series, issue and credits (press Y). No ComicInfo? Press X on the details card to look the book up in the Grand Comics Database.
