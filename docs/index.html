Comic Reader!

Read comics and manuals on your cabinet. Pages on the playfield, the cover on the backglass, the page you're on on the DMD.

Three screensThe whole cabinet

PlayfieldYour library, then the pages at full size: 2160 pixels wide on a 4KP.
BackglassThe book's cover.
DMDThe title and the page you're on.

ReadingPow! Zoom! Turn!

Read

Flippers turn the page; hold one to flip fast. Your place is kept in every book, and Continue lists the ones you're partway through.

Page curl

The page folds over, print showing faintly through the back. Or a slide, or none: your pick in Settings.

Zoom

Start zooms to 2x and 3x; the joystick moves around, and the text sharpens when you stop. Great for small print.

Details

Y shows a book's series, story, date, credits and summary, from its ComicInfo.xml, or looked up in the Grand Comics Database.

Settings

Wi-Fi upload, updates from GitHub, the page turn, right-to-left reading for manga, and an attract mode: left alone, comics read themselves.

Wi-Fi upload

Send books from a phone or computer: open the address the cabinet shows, enter its PIN, drop the files in.

Get itInstall

  1. Download comic-reader-v…zip from the latest release.
  2. Unzip it and copy its external folder to the root of your cabinet's USB stick.
  3. Comic Reader shows up with the other External Apps.
  4. Later versions: Settings > Check for updates.
  • Opens: PDF, CBZ, CBR, CBT, single pictures (JPG, PNG, WebP, GIF, BMP, TIFF) and folders of pictures.
  • Books go in external/comic-reader/comics/ or manuals/; a folder of books is a series.
  • Try it out: the release has an optional pack of public-domain Golden Age comics, one of each file type.

Comic Reader ships no copyrighted books: bring your own. Read the guide for every button.
